How it works
1
💻
Set up the laptop
Log in with your name to get a permanent room code. Download the Chrome extension and enter your room code once — it stays connected.
2
📱
Connect a phone
Scan the QR code on this page — or open scan-to-fill.onrender.com/mobile.html and type the room code. Multiple phones can join the same room.
3
📷
Scan the paper form
The scanner taps Take Photo and photographs the patient's paper intake form. Claude AI reads the handwriting and extracts every field automatically.
4
✏️
Review & confirm
The phone shows all extracted fields. The scanner checks for mistakes, edits any wrong values, then taps Confirm & Fill Form.
5
✅
Form fills itself
The NCNMO web form on the laptop fills in automatically — name, gender, marital status, state, location, date of birth, everything. No clicking needed.
6
🔁
Next patient, instantly
The laptop navigates to a fresh blank form on its own. The scanner immediately photographs the next patient's form. The cycle repeats.
Chrome Extension Setup
⬇ Download Extension
  1. Download and unzip the file above
  2. Go to chrome://extensions in Chrome
  3. Enable Developer mode (top-right toggle)
  4. Click Load unpacked → select the unzipped folder
  5. Click the extension icon → enter room code —
  6. Click a scan below → then hit Fill Page Now in the extension
