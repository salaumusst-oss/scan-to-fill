How it works

1

Install the extension

Load the Chrome extension on your laptop. It connects to this server automatically.

2

Set your room code

Open the extension → Settings. Pick a short code (e.g. "JANE"). Each person at your team gets their own code.

3

Open scanner on phone

Enter your room code below and tap "Open Scanner". Bookmark that page on your phone.

4

Scan & fill

Take a photo of the patient form. AI reads it. Click "Fill Page Now" in the extension to fill the form.

Open the phone scanner

This opens the scanner page for your room. Open it on your phone.
